I finally have time to sit down and reflect on my March mission trip to Africa.

One story that deeply moved me was about a Chinese lady named Pei Pei

She was from China, I met her last November, I share
the gospel and my testimony to her, but at that time I don’t had the boldness
to ask her to receive Jesus as her Lord and Savior, but I promised her, If I
get to come to Africa again next year March, I will bring a Chinese bible for
her, during this time I also pray for another person that speaks Chinese will
come with me to Africa to share Jesus with her.

So this year March, God brought my friend Darius, he is born in America and been learning for a year of Chinese on YouTube, we went to the same restaurant Pei Pei worked, and we found her, she is surprised we came to visit her again, and we share the gospel again
to her this time, and asked her if she want to received Jesus as her Lord and
Savior, she said yes! We pray together and we give her Bible in Chinese!


我終於有時間靜下心來，回顧我三月的非洲短宣之旅。

有一個故事深深感動了我，是關於一位名叫佩佩的中國女生。


她來自中國湖北，我在去年十一月第一次遇見她，那時我向她分享了福音和我的見證。但那時我沒有勇氣邀請她接受耶穌成為她的救主。但我向她承諾，如果我明年三月有機會再次來到非洲，我會帶一本中文聖經給她。這段期間，我也一直在禱告，希望有另一位會說中文的人能與我一起來非洲，向她傳福音。

到了今年三月，神真的帶領我的朋友 戴維斯 一起來。他是在美國出生的，他花路一年的時間在 YouTube 上學中文。我們一起去了佩佩工作的那家餐廳，真的找到她了。她很驚訝我們再次來看她。我們再次向她分享福音，這次我們鼓起勇氣問她是否願意接受耶穌成為她的主和救主。她說：「我願意！」我們一起禱告，並送給她一本中文聖經！


Another story that really moved me was about two waiters at our hotel. We’ve seen them several times on past mission trips

\
Mariama was fasting in Ramadan, and she is a Muslim. We share the gospel with
her, but she was busy working, so didn’t get the chance to fully ask her to
make the decision to follow Jesus that night. The next morning, we see her, and
she said she spoke with her dad and think about the whole night what we
discussed last night. She decides on her own she want to give her life to Jesus
Christ and later we baptize her at the beach, she was so joyful and happy. What
happened was we were sharing with her cousin Leonora first, later after she
said she want to believe God, we ask her do you have anyone here in the
restaurant you want to share Jesus to? She points to her cousin Mariama.\
\

另一個讓我很感動的故事，是關於我們在我們住的旅館遇到的兩位服務員。我們在過去幾次的宣教之旅中，都在這個旅館遇見過他們。

其中一位叫 Mariama，她是一位穆斯林，當時正在齋戒月中禁食。我們跟她分享了福音，但她工作很忙，當晚沒機會作出決定。隔天早上，我們又見到她。她告訴我們，昨晚回去之後跟她爸爸聊過，也整夜在思考我們分享談論的內容。最後她自己做出決定，要把生命交給耶穌。後來我們在海邊為她施洗，她的臉上充滿了喜樂與幸福。

原來，我們一開始是先向她的表妹 Leonora 傳福音。當 Leonora 說她想相信神之後，我們問她：「你在這餐廳裡有沒有想分享耶穌的人？」她馬上指向她的表妹 Mariama。

This coming July, God is bringing one of my close friend who speaks Chinese to join the mission trip, Vivian. She has a deep desire to go to Africa God really touched her and lit a fire inside of her , and I’m just overwhelmed with joy. God truly hears our prayers.

He cares about the Chinese people living in Sierra Leone. Many of them have come here to work as coal miners and fishermen, often spending at least six months away from their wive and children back in China. But even in a foreign land, God sees them. He loves them. He hasn’t forgotten them.

I believe it’s no accident, He placed this burden in both my heart and Vivian’s, to go and share Jesus with them in their heart language.

So fam, please pray for us, Pray that God will open the door for us to go. Pray for His provision. Pray that He will send us to touch the lives of Chinese people in Africa with His love. I love you all!





今年七月，神感動了我的一個會說中文的好朋友 惠敏，一起參加這次宣教之旅。她心中有一個深切的渴望要去非洲傳福音去孤兒院 監獄 紅燈區 部落 傳福音給華人 跟非洲人，神真的感動了她，也在她心裡點燃了一把火。我感到無比喜樂，因為神真的是聽禱告的神帶了會說中文的來了非洲。


祂關心那些住在獅子山的中國人。他們中有許多人來這裡工作，從事礦工和漁夫的職業，往往必須離開妻子和孩子，在異鄉連續工作至少六個月。但即使身在異地，神仍然看顧他們，愛他們，沒有忘記他們。


我相信這一切絕非偶然。神把這樣的負擔放在我和 惠敏 的心中，要我們去用他們熟悉的語言，向他們分享耶穌。




親愛的家人們，請為我們禱告：為神開路，讓我們可以順利前往。為祂會為我們供應所有經費禱告。讓祂差派我們，去觸摸非洲人中國人的心靈，讓他們感受到耶穌的愛。




我愛你們，謝謝你們的代禱與支持！
